# ADR-002 — Trainer X Persona + Hybrid Agent Model

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Decision date:** 2026-09-24

## Decision

Expose one primary user persona: **X / Trainer X**.

Training, recovery, general nutrition information and motivation begin as internal skills/capabilities of X.

Support and Community may be separate agents because their permissions, channel behavior and escalation rules differ.

## Reason

A single primary persona avoids fragmented UX while preserving internal specialization.

## Safety boundary

X is not a physician, physiotherapist or clinical nutritionist and must not diagnose, prescribe medication, infer disease or present unsafe extreme protocols.
