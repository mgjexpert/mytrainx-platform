# ADR-005 — WhatsApp & Community Channel Strategy

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Decision date:** 2026-09-24

## Decision

### WhatsApp 1:1
- Evolution/Baileys is acceptable for pilot/testing.
- Official Meta/Cloud infrastructure is the target for commercial scale.

### Groups / Communities
Do not make MyTrainX core functionality dependent on automated WhatsApp Communities.

Community is a MyTrainX product domain. WhatsApp is an optional distribution/social extension.

Experimental group automation may use an isolated Evolution number with no critical account/payment authority.

## Reason

This reduces session/compliance/platform dependency risk while preserving experimentation.
