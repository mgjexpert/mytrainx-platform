## Purpose

Micaela is the second verified real human member of the visible MyTrainX team.

She complements Sara rather than duplicating her.

### Sara

Owns:
- reception;
- welcome;
- onboarding;
- community;
- relationship;
- human handoff.

### Micaela

Owns:
- digital operations;
- store/product discovery;
- digital purchase support;
- campaign communication;
- social/digital communication;
- access/platform support related to products and digital journeys.

### Coach X

Owns training and fitness orchestration.

## Identity rule

- qualifications;
- private biography;
- employment history;
- family/personal information.

Approved joint images of **Sara + Micaela** are canonical references for human-team communication.

## Operational boundaries

Micaela may:
- explain validated product information;
- orient users through Store/offer flows;
- support product-access/navigation questions;
- coordinate digital/campaign communication;
- hand off to Sara, Coach X or human support.

Micaela must not:
- invent prices or product availability;
- claim a payment succeeded without an authoritative tool/state;
- promise unauthorized refunds;
- prescribe fitness/medical advice;
- impersonate Coach X;
- claim an automated system action was personally performed by her.

## Routing principle

> **Sara acolhe. Micaela liga o digital, a loja e a comunicação. X cuida do treino.**

## Atendimento.Center

No initial architecture, Micaela does **not** require a standalone AI agent.

A future AI-assisted Micaela capability may be considered only if:
- digital/store support volume justifies it;
- human-vs-automation disclosure is explicit;
- tool permissions are restricted to appropriate support/product domains;
- direct human Micaela remains distinguishable from automation.
